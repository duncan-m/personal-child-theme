A worked calculation

Fee = payment value × percentage fee + fixed fee

£100 × 1.5% + £0.20 = £1.70

Assumes a single successful £100 payment, no additional fees and illustrative pricing.

A comparison

Authorisation
The issuer approves or declines the request. Approval is distinct from settlement.
Settlement
Funds move according to the payment arrangement and applicable timing.
Idempotency
Repeated requests with the same key should not create duplicate operations within the provider’s documented behaviour.
Reporting cohort
A defined group of transactions observed over the same period.Record the date range, population and maturity.
